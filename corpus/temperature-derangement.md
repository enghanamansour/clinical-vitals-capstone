# NEWS2 temperature bands

Temperature of 35.0 C or below scores 3. From 35.1 to 36.0 scores 1. The normal
band 36.1 to 38.0 scores 0. From 38.1 to 39.0 scores 1, and 39.1 or above scores
2. Both extremes matter: a low temperature in an unwell patient can be a sign of
severe sepsis and carries a worse prognosis than fever.

# Fever

A new fever with signs of acute illness should prompt a search for a source:
respiratory, urinary, abdominal, skin and soft tissue, central nervous system,
and device or line related. Take cultures before antibiotics where possible.
Antipyretics improve comfort but do not treat the cause. A fever with a NEWS2 of
5 or more, rigors, or haemodynamic instability should trigger a sepsis screen.

# Hypothermia

For a core temperature below 35 C, remove wet clothing, use warmed blankets and a
forced-air warming device, give warmed intravenous fluids, and monitor
continuously with a low-reading thermometer. Handle the patient gently because
the cold myocardium is prone to arrhythmia. Look for causes: environmental
exposure, sepsis, hypothyroidism, hypoglycaemia, adrenal insufficiency, and
drugs. Rewarm before concluding a neurological assessment.
