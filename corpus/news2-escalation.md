# What NEWS2 measures

NEWS2 aggregates seven parameters into a single score: respiratory rate, oxygen
saturation, use of supplemental oxygen, systolic blood pressure, pulse rate,
level of consciousness on the ACVPU scale, and temperature. Each parameter
contributes 0 to 3 points and the points are summed. A score of 3 in any single
parameter is clinically significant on its own, even when the total is low.

# Escalation thresholds

- **Total 0**: continue routine monitoring, minimum 12-hourly observations.
- **Total 1-4 (low)**: ward-based response. Increase observation frequency to a
  minimum of 4-6 hourly and inform the registered nurse.
- **Single parameter scoring 3 (low-medium)**: registered nurse to review and
  decide whether to escalate. Increase observations to at least hourly.
- **Total 5-6 (medium)**: urgent review by a clinician competent in assessing
  acute illness. Consider whether the patient needs continuous monitoring and
  escalate to critical care outreach.
- **Total 7 or more (high)**: emergency assessment by a critical care team,
  usually transfer to a higher-care environment, and continuous monitoring of
  vital signs.

# Oxygen and the NEWS2 SpO2 scale

Use SpO2 Scale 1 for most patients (target 94-98%). Use SpO2 Scale 2 only for
patients with confirmed hypercapnic respiratory failure and a target of 88-92%,
and only when prescribed. A patient on supplemental oxygen always scores 2 for
the oxygen parameter regardless of the measured saturation.

# Documentation

Record the individual parameter scores, the aggregate, the time, the escalation
action taken, and the responder. A rising trend across consecutive observation
sets matters as much as any single value.
